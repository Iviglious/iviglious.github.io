# Mortgage Lump-Sum Optimizer

Simple interactive webpage to explore how a one-time lump-sum payment affects mortgage interest and term.

Usage:

-- Open [mortgage-pay/index.html](mortgage-pay/index.html) in a browser.
-- Adjust `Principal` (in £), `Annual Rate`, and `Term`.
-- Drag the `Lump sum` slider to preview interest saved and remaining months.
-- The app suggests a minimal lump achieving the configured target percent (default 90%) of the maximum possible interest savings.
